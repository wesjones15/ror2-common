Optional: put your own GameCube disc images in this folder (or one folder inside it).
All three mods work without discs or extracted assets, using built-in gameplay and generic shapes.
Missing custom arrival assets use RoR2's standard drop pod; missing game audio/effects are omitted.

    .iso, .gcm or .ciso. Any file names work: each mod reads the disc itself to find its game.
    Convert .rvz, .wia or .wbfs images to .iso first (Dolphin: right-click the game, Convert File).

Which discs the mods use:

    Mario mod   Super Mario Sunshine (any region)                      optional: original presentation and tuning
                Super Smash Bros. Melee (USA, revision 1.02)           optional: Luigi skin, punch and kick
    Olimar mod  Pikmin (any region)                                    optional: original presentation and tuning
                Pikmin 2 (any region)                                  optional: Louie, more enemies, treasures
                Super Smash Bros. Melee (USA, revision 1.02)           optional: Captain Pikachu skin
    Melee mod   Super Smash Bros. Melee (USA, revision 1.02)           optional: original presentation and tuning

On the first launch each mod copies the files it needs from your discs into the assets folder next to this one.
After that the discs aren't read again; you can keep them here or move them away.

Nothing from these discs is included with the mods, and you should never share the assets folder.
